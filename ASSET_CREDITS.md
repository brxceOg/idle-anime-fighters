# Asset credits

## Integrated fighter sprite artwork
- **Kai Ren — Martial Hero** by LuizMelo. CC0 1.0 Universal; freely usable and modifiable, including commercially. Original pack: https://luizmelo.itch.io/martial-hero. The animation PNGs were sourced from the public example project https://github.com/CodeWritingCow/javascript-fighting-game and copied into this game's assets/fighters/kai/ directory with normalized filenames.
- **Raika — Martial Hero 2** by LuizMelo. CC0 1.0 Universal; freely usable and modifiable, including commercially. Original pack: https://luizmelo.itch.io/martial-hero-2. The animation PNGs were sourced from the public example project https://github.com/CodeWritingCow/javascript-fighting-game and copied into this game's assets/fighters/raika/ directory with normalized filenames.
- **Yurei — Huntress** by LuizMelo. CC0 1.0 Universal; freely usable and modifiable, including commercially. Original pack: https://luizmelo.itch.io/huntress. The animation PNGs were sourced from https://github.com/alelthomas/2d-jungle-game, which identifies the Huntress art as LuizMelo's work, and copied into this game's assets/fighters/yurei/ directory with normalized filenames.
- All three sets include genuine frame sequences for their listed animation states. The game's combat simulation, sprite timing, stage artwork, HUD, portraits, and VFX integration are project-authored.

## Arena and interface artwork
- **Iron Dojo stage, arcade HUD, portrait frames, health/energy meters, hit effects, and energy projectile effects:** original project-authored canvas/CSS artwork inspired by classic MUGEN and arcade fighting-game presentation.

## Asset notes
- The current fighter art uses CC0 sprite packs, not extracted commercial anime/game character rips or native MUGEN SFF archives.
- The assets are bundled in the public repository because the source packs are marked CC0 1.0 Universal. Attribution is not required by CC0, but is included here to credit the artists and document the source projects.
