## MUGEN / community asset direction
The project may use MUGEN character packs and community fighting-game assets for private concept evaluation when the source files are supplied or can be lawfully obtained. Keep each pack's author, source, and license recorded here. Personal testing does not automatically grant permission to redistribute copyrighted packs in a public repository, so do not commit third-party packs unless their terms permit it or the user has confirmed the required rights.
